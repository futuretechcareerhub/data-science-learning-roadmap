What this checklist is for

Searching for data science training in India can bring up programs with different syllabuses, formats and levels. Before comparing providers, it helps to understand your own starting point. This free checklist is designed as a self-assessment—not a certification, admission test or promise of employment.

Use it to mark the skills you can demonstrate independently, identify gaps and prepare specific questions for any training provider you consider. The goal is to select a learning path that matches your experience instead of choosing only by a course title.

How to use it: for each statement, mark “Can do”, “Need practice” or “Not started”. Be honest and use evidence from your own code, exercises or projects.

6. Project and portfolio readiness

  • Can you explain the problem statement and why it matters?
  • Can another person reproduce your analysis from the repository instructions?
  • Have you documented the data source, cleaning steps and assumptions?
  • Do you explain the evaluation results and project limitations?
  • Can you summarize your findings without relying only on notebook output?

A portfolio is evidence of what you can do, not simply a count of repositories. The Data Science Portfolio Guide covers project documentation and presentation.

There is no universal score that determines whether someone is ready for a data science job or course. Use the checklist to find the next skill to practice, and revisit it as you complete projects.

Choosing data science training in India

When comparing data science training in India, use your checklist to ask providers specific questions: which modules cover your gaps, what practical work is assessed, how feedback is delivered, and what project outcomes you can expect to demonstrate. Compare the published syllabus and support details with your own goals.

If you are considering a structured program, review Data Science Training in India at TechPratham and compare its curriculum, project practice and career-oriented components against the areas in this checklist. Frequently asked questions

What skills should I learn before data science training?

Prior knowledge of basic mathematics can help, but many beginner pathways start with Python and statistics. Check the prerequisites of each specific program.

Is this checklist a certification or entrance test?

No. It is a self-assessment worksheet for personal learning planning, not a formal exam, credential or admission requirement.

What is the difference between data science training and data analytics training?

There is overlap. Data analytics often emphasizes querying, reporting, visualization and business insights. Data science commonly adds statistical modeling and machine learning, depending on the role and curriculum.

Does completing training guarantee a data science job?

No course or checklist can guarantee employment. Outcomes depend on demonstrated skills, project work, experience, interview performance and the hiring requirements of individual employers.

How often should I repeat this assessment?

Repeat it after completing a learning module or project, and update your answers based on work you can demonstrate independently.

Summary

Use this free readiness checklist to identify skill gaps, organize your learning and ask better questions when comparing training options. A practical data science learning plan should connect Python, statistics, SQL, data analysis, machine learning and project communication in a sequence that fits your current level.
